The room

One screen for what you follow

Arrange news search, RSS, maps, markets, and wire feeds on a single workspace. Pause live updates when you need focus. Share a read-only room when your team needs the same picture.

You choose the sources. Vigil gathers them in one place so you stop chasing tabs.

The brief

A cited digest from your room

Generate a short brief from the headlines already on your screen. Each point links back to the item it came from. Export as text or PDF, or email it to yourself.

The brief summarizes what your room surfaced. It does not add facts the feeds did not carry.

The alerts

Keywords you set, delivered calmly

Define alert rules on the topics you watch. New matches appear in an in-app activity feed. Paid plans can add email and team webhook delivery on a daily cadence.

Alerts notify you when something new matches. They do not assert that a story is verified.

Who it is for

Built for people who watch the world

Newsrooms, field teams, and analysts use Vigil when they need one composed view of fast-moving information. The room stays neutral. You bring the judgment.
